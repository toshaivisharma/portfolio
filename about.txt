PROJECTS
==============================

1. ECONOMY VS. YOUR ECONOMY
Type: interactive dashboard
Data: FRED (Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis), 2000 to today
Link: https://economy-vs-your-economy.streamlit.app/
The headline economy is stock prices and GDP. Your economy is your household's income, your rent and your groceries. Using FRED data from the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis, this dashboard shows how far the two have drifted apart since 2000: after inflation the Nasdaq is up 242%, while median household income is up 11%.

2. ECONOMY FEELS FINE
Type: interactive data story
Link: https://economy-feels-fine.vercel.app/
Economy feels fine is a field of hundreds of coloured dots, and every one of them is a moment in the economy. Hover or tap any dot to read what happened.

3. MEDIA BIAS CHART
Type: interactive chart
Sources: 76
Link: https://media-bias-chart.vercel.app/index.html
Every outlet tells the story a little differently. The media bias chart sorts 76 of the best-known news sources by their political lean, from left to right, so you can see where your own news sits.

4. MY DIORAMA
Type: website, art
Made: 2026
Link: https://my-diorama.vercel.app/
My diorama is a small online gallery of art I have made from childhood to now. The pieces hang on a dark wall under track lights, and you can step back, zoom in and look at each one up close.

5. THE BIG 1
Type: case competition deck (15 slides)
Team: Shaivi Sharma, Hassan Ali, Sameeksha Dhandhania, Aayan Mallhi
The big 1 is a case pitch written as PwC, built around building trust in society and solving important problems. It walks through what was at risk for the client, from corporate development disruption to international tax complexity and an ERP migration, and how we would solve it while keeping full audit independence.

ABOUT ME (from the homepage)
==============================

Hi, I'm Shaivi! I made this website to bring my work and my creative side into one place, and I'm grateful you're taking the time to get to know me a little better.

In a nutshell, I'm an Economics and Finance Honors student at Drexel University, based in Philadelphia and originally from India. Most of my work comes down to finding where something gets stuck and fixing it. At GoTrust in New Delhi, I learned how a startup decides what to build and who to build it for, and how much depends on listening to customers before pitching to them. Chubb was my first co-op and my first time inside a large company, where I learned how one small mismatch in the data can slow down an entire team, and how much better the work gets when you stop and ask why a number looks off. Competitions gave me the same puzzle on a deadline, at the College Fed Challenge as one of two freshmen on Drexel's team and at PwC's case competition, where I captained the winning team. Next, I want to bring that to wealth management or consulting, where a good answer only works if the client understands it.

Writing keeps me sharp at that part. I write opinion pieces for The Triangle and lead an editorial team at the Perrin Institute, and on Substack I write about my own life instead. I also lead Drexel's Climate Cardinals chapter, where 50 volunteers teach climate literacy across campus, and I designed almost everything on this site myself.
