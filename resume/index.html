I love the intersection of economics, data, and storytelling, and I like taking a messy process and making it actually work.

study

Currently, I’m studying Economics & Finance (Honors) at Drexel UniversityD, class of 2028, with a minor in international business and product design.

I’m an A.J. Drexel Scholar on the Dean’s List, and some favorite classes so far have been game theory, econometrics, and economics & big data.

experience

Most recently, I was a co-op on the global treaty controllership team at ChubbC, living in the back office of reinsurance: reconciling premium against the ledger, chasing down why the numbers didn’t match, and rebuilding our bordereaux workflows in Alteryx so settlement across APAC and Europe stopped taking forever.

Before that, I was a product management intern at GoTrustG in New Delhi, working on a data privacy compliance platform for India’s new privacy law, figuring out which customers needed it most and how to show it to them. I also spent a year as a research fund scholar at Drexel, cleaning and analyzing teaching data in Excel and Stata.

competitions & leadership

I captained our team to first place at the PwC Challenge case competition, pitching an ESG scoring framework and an M&A advisory recommendation.

I’m chapter president of Climate CardinalsCC, where I turned a big, loose group into small teams with owners and actually-shipped campus projects. I’m also on the Economics Society e‑board and was a USGA freshman senator.

On the side, I wrote a short thesis on monday.com, building the DCF and comps myself and arguing the market had it priced too high.
