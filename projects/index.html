economy vs. your economy

data:
FRED, 2000 to today
type:
interactive dashboard

The headline economy is stock prices and GDP. Your economy is your household's income, your rent and your groceries. Using FRED data from the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis, this dashboard shows how far the two have drifted apart since 2000: after inflation the Nasdaq is up 242%, while median household income is up 11%.
